The library sits under the decks. The sidebar on the left picks what the list
shows; the list on the right is your tracks.

## The sidebar

| Section | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| **LIBRARY ▸ All Tracks** | Your collection. The number next to it is how many tracks it holds. |
| **SMART CRATES** | **Recently Played**, **Session History** and **Just Imported**. Dub fills these by itself. |
| **DUB CRATES** | The crates you make in Dub. |
| **IMPORTED SOURCES** | Serato, Traktor, rekordbox and Apple Music, with their playlists. See [Bring your library](/library/import). |
| **REAL RECORDS** | The record sides you've ripped. |

Above the list is a strip of eight favourite slots. Drag a crate or a
playlist from the sidebar onto a slot, then click the slot to open it.
Right-click a slot and choose **Clear Slot** to empty it.

## Search

Type in **Search** at the top of the sidebar. The list narrows as you type.
The clear button at the end of the field empties it.

## Filter

The **FILTER** bar above the list holds a box per field: **Genre**, **BPM**,
**Key** and **Rating** to start with. Tick values in a box to show only
those tracks. Each value shows how many tracks match.

- **BPM** splits the list into tempo ranges, such as `124–128`.
- **Rating** shows tracks with at least the stars you click.
- Tracks with an empty field collect under **(none)**.

Add or remove boxes with the **+** at the right of the bar. Click the bar's
line to fold it away; folded, it still says what you're filtering by. Click
**Clear** to drop every filter.

## Columns and sorting

**Artist** and **Title** are always there. Right-click a column header to
show or hide the others:

- **Analysis**: **Length**, **BPM**, **Key**
- **ID3 metadata**: **Album**, **Genre**, **Year**, **Comment**,
  **Composer**, **Track #**
- **Library**: **Rating**, **Color**, **Version**, **Source**
- **More columns**: everything else, grouped. Each app you imported from
  gets its own group with its own BPM, key and tags, so you can put
  **Serato BPM** next to **BPM (auto)** and compare. There are also groups
  for the audio file (codec, rate, size, path) and for your **Mix history**
  (**Plays**, **Last played**).

Drag a header to move a column. Click a header to sort: once ascending,
twice descending, a third time to turn the sort off. Empty cells always go
to the bottom.

The **Key** column is in Camelot, coloured around the wheel so compatible
keys stand out down the list. Right-click a header and choose **Toggle Key
Notation (Musical)** to read it as `C major` instead.

## Crates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make a crate">
    Click the **+** next to **DUB CRATES** and type a name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it">
    Drag tracks from the list onto the crate in the sidebar. A file dragged
    from the Finder joins only if it's already in your library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put it in order">
    Open the crate. The **#** column is its running order; sort by it and
    drag rows to reorder. Or right-click a track: **Move Up**, **Move
    Down**, **Move to Top**, **Move to Bottom**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Right-click a crate for **Rename**, **Export As…** and **Delete**. Deleting a
crate removes only the list. The tracks stay in your library. Inside a
crate, **Remove from Crate** takes the selected tracks out.

Playlists under **IMPORTED SOURCES** are read-only. They show what the other
app has; you can't rename, reorder or drop into them.

## Ratings and colours

Turn on the **Rating** and **Color** columns.

- **Rating**: click a star to rate from one to five. Click the same star
  again to clear it.
- **Color**: click the swatch and pick **Red**, **Orange**, **Yellow**,
  **Green**, **Aqua**, **Blue**, **Purple** or **Pink**, or **None**. The
  whole row takes a tint of the colour.

## What you played

Dub records what each deck loads and plays. It never sees your mixer, so it
works out a transition from the decks: when one deck stops while the other
keeps playing a different track, and the outgoing track played for at least
30 seconds, that counts as a mix from one into the other.

- **Recently Played** lists tracks you've loaded on a deck, newest first.
- **Session History** is the set you've played since you opened Dub. A track you
  mixed into shows **← from** and the track before it.
- When you load a track you've mixed out of before, the deck header shows
  **↝ usually:** and the track you most often play next. Click it to find
  that track in the library, then press Space to load it.

## Analysis

Dub reads tempo, beat grid and key from the audio itself. It does this
lazily: importing stays fast, and a track is analysed the first time you
load it. The deck plays straight away and the **BPM** fills in a few
seconds later. While it works, the bottom-right corner of the list shows
**Analyzing 1 track…**.

To analyse ahead of a gig, select tracks, right-click and choose **Analyze**
(or **Re-analyze** for tracks done before). The menu counts what it will
do: **Re-analyze Selected (3 of 5)** means two of the five have a locked
grid and are skipped.

Marks in the **BPM** column:

| Mark | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **⚠** *Imported and analysed grids disagree* | Another app's grid and Dub's analysis don't match. Show the per-app BPM columns to compare. |
| **⚠** *May drift over a long mix* | The grid may wander late in the track. Right-click ▸ **Lock grid** to accept it. |
| Padlock | The grid is locked. Analysis leaves it alone. Right-click ▸ **Unlock grid** to free it. |

Hover a **⚠** to see which one it is.

Key comes out as Camelot. A grid from another app stays in charge: Dub's own
reading sits beside it in **BPM (auto)** and **Key (auto)**.

## Duplicates

When you import, Dub fingerprints each file. A file that sounds the same as a
track you already have, with the same length, joins that track as a second
copy rather than a second row.

Dub won't merge versions. A title or filename marked `(Clean)`, `(Dirty)`,
`(Instrumental)`, `(Acapella)`, `(Radio Edit)`, `(Extended Mix)` or
`(12" Mix)` stays its own track, and the **Version** column shows the tag.
Versions of the same recording get a link icon in the row. Click it to jump to the other version.

## Missing files

A track on a drive that's unplugged shows a red triangle in its row. When
files can't be found, the bottom-right corner of the list counts them, for
example **12 missing**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Relocate">
    Click the missing count. **Relocate Missing Files** opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point Dub at the files">
    Click **Match Folder…** and choose the folder that now holds them. Dub
    matches files by fingerprint and length, so renamed files are found
    too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    The sheet reports how many it relocated. Anything still missing can be
    tried against another folder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Dub never deletes a track because its file went missing. Your grids, cues,
ratings and history stay, and when the drive comes back the old path works
again.
