The rack bar under the decks holds the **SAMPLES** shelf and the **DUB SIREN**
box. The chevron at its left edge folds it away so the library gets the
space; click the folded strip to bring it back.

<Note>
Nothing here ships with a key. Click **MAP** in the status strip, click a pad
or a siren shot, then press the key you want on it.
</Note>

## The sampler

Eight slots, 1–4 over 5–8. The heading counts what's loaded: **3 OF 8**. The
same shelf appears in **Prep**, so a slot you load there is the pad you fire
here. Samples stay loaded between launches.

### Loading

Drag a track from the library or from Finder onto a slot. Dropping onto a full
slot replaces it. Empty slots are dashed outlines with their number.

### Playing

| Do this | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Click | Plays the sample once, through to the end. |
| Click while it sounds | Starts it over. |
| Right-click while it sounds | Stops it. |
| Right-click while quiet | **Quick Scratch 1**–**4**, **Rename…**, **Unload**. |

A sounding pad lights and a sweep crosses it, so you can see how much is
left. Samples play on top of the deck, never instead of it, and each one is
levelled for loudness when it loads.

**Rename…** gives the pad your own name; leave it blank to go back to the
filename. Replacing the sample clears the name.

### Where samples sound

The **→ A** pill on the heading shows the deck the samples land on: the master
deck, by default. Right-click it to pin them:

- **Auto — follows the master deck**
- **Deck A**
- **Deck B**
- **A + B**

### MISSING and UNREADABLE

**MISSING** means the file was moved or deleted. **UNREADABLE** means it's
there but can't be decoded. The pad keeps its place and name; drop another
file on it. See
[Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#a-sample-or-quick-scratch-pad-says-missing).

## Quick Scratch

Quick Scratch puts a sample on the deck, under the needle, and parks the tune.
You scratch the sample with the turntable; press again and the tune is back.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tag a sample">
    Right-click a quiet SAMPLES tile and pick **Quick Scratch 1**–**4**. The
    tile shows **QS1**–**QS4**. A tag sits on one slot only, so tagging
    another slot moves it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press the pad on the deck">
    Each deck column has a **SCRATCH** row of four pads, one per tag. Press
    one and its sample is on that deck from the start. The title reads
    **SCRATCH** and the line under it shows the parked tune and where it is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press it again">
    The tune comes back. Press a different pad instead and the sample swaps;
    the tune stays parked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Where the tune waits depends on the decks:

- **This deck playing, the other idle**: the tune is doubled onto the other
  deck and keeps playing there. Get that channel's fader up before you
  press. On release this deck takes the tune back in sync, and the other deck
  returns to what it had.
- **Both decks playing**: the tune runs on silently underneath and comes back
  in time.
- **This deck paused**: the tune comes back to the exact frame.

Loading a track onto the deck, switching it to THRU, or doubling onto it
drops the park. A loop on the tune doesn't survive the park.

Right-click a Quick Scratch pad while it's off the deck for **Rename…**; it
renames the SAMPLES slot behind it. A pad whose file is missing tells you
which file is gone and won't park your tune for silence.

## The dub siren

Five shots, each on a pad drawn with its own waveform: **Rifle Gun**,
**Alarm**, **Sine**, **Laser**, **Siren**. Click a pad to fire it. While it
sounds, the heading names the shot.

The siren is its own instrument with its own echo. It sounds with or without a
track loaded, and echo out on the deck doesn't touch it.

### The DUB dial

Drag the dial up or down. As it turns it slows and deepens the shots and adds
the siren's echo: longer, louder and darker. **DRY** is no echo at all;
double-click the dial to get back there. The dial reads **DUB** and a
percentage, and it's remembered between launches.

The **TAIL** row under the pads shows the echo the dial has set: one segment
per repeat, fading as the repeats do, with the delay in ms and the feedback in
% beside it.

### Beat-matching the echo

Turn on **Beat-match the siren's echo** in **Preferences ▸ FX**. The echo then
repeats once per beat at the deck's tempo (or every half beat when a beat is
longer than the echo can hold) instead of following the dial's time.

### Where the siren goes

The **→ A** pill works like the sampler's: the master deck by default, or
right-click to pin **Deck A**, **Deck B** or **A + B**. The siren leaves on
that deck's output, into that channel of your mixer.

Hide the box with **Dub siren** in **Preferences ▸ FX**.
