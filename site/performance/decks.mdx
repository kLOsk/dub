![The Performance surface: two deck columns either side of the playing strips](/images/performance.png)

Each deck has a column beside its waveform strip. Top to bottom: the source
switch, the title and readouts, the track overview, the hot cues, then one row
of **SCRATCH**, **LOOP** and **ECHO**.

The source switch (**INT · TC · THRU**), calibration and the **SIGNAL** panel
are covered in [Your first set](/first-set#the-source-switch-int-tc-thru).

<Note>
Nothing you play ships with a key. Click **MAP** in the status strip, click a
control, then press the key you want on it. Only **Space** (load) and **⌘,**
(Preferences) are bound out of the box.
</Note>

## The readouts

- **MASTER** sits in the deck's top corner. It marks the deck the siren and
  samples land on, and the one Space-load avoids.
- **BPM** is the tempo the record is playing at right now: the track's tempo
  scaled by your pitch. Ride the pitch until the two BPMs agree.
- **KEY** is the track's key in Camelot.
- **PITCH** follows the platter, in percent.
- **LOCK** is key lock. **ON**, the key holds while the platter moves the
  tempo; **OFF**, pitch rides the platter like a record. Its dot is dim while
  there's nothing to hold (at +0.0 % or inside a loop) and solid once it's
  working.

Elapsed and remaining time sit above the overview. Click the overview to jump
anywhere in the track; a paused deck stays paused.

## The strip and zoom

The playing waveform runs vertically, with the playhead a quarter of the way
down. Drag the strip to cue by hand: pushing it up plays forward, holding
still is a stopped record.

Hover over the strips and a **+** / **−** pair appears in the gap between
them. It zooms both decks together, from **0.25×** to **2×**, so the two
strips always share a scale.

## The phase meter

The bar between the decks is one beat tall, centred on the playhead line. Its
marker is the other deck's beat against the master's, labelled **A** or **B**.

- Marker **above** the line: that deck is late. Push the record.
- Marker **below** the line: it's early.
- The marker turns **green** when both decks play within 15 ms of each other.

The meter only shows phase. Match tempo by the BPM readouts first. Both decks
need a beat grid for the marker to appear.

## Beat grid and set the 1

The **BPM** readout is a tap pad, in Performance as in [Prep](/prep):

- **1–2 taps** on the downbeat set the 1. The grid moves its 1 to the kick
  you tapped; the tempo stays.
- **3 or more taps** on the beat re-take the tempo, with the first tap as
  the 1. While you tap, the label counts them: **BPM (3)**.

Right-click it for **2×**, **½**, **Reset to auto** and **Lock grid**. A locked
grid shows a padlock by **BPM** and takes no taps until you unlock it. Every
fix is saved with the track.

To tap from the keyboard, map a key to the BPM in [map mode](/map-mode).

<Note>
**Alpha.** With **Following beat grid** on in **Preferences ▸ Alpha**, a
track played by a band gets a line on each beat where the band played it. The
readout then says **BPM · FOLLOW** and moves as the track plays.
</Note>

### A real record on THRU

A THRU deck has no track, so its BPM is the record's tempo, heard live. The
**TEMPO** heading says how sure it is: **○ LISTENING**, **◐ TENTATIVE**,
**● LOCKED**.

- **Tap the BPM on the 1** (once or twice) and the strip draws a bar line on
  that beat and every fourth beat after it.
- **Tap it 3 or more times** on the beat to set the tempo yourself
  (**● YOUR TAP**). The first tap is the 1.
- **Right-click the BPM** for **Double — ×2**, **Half — ÷2**,
  **×1.5 — drum & bass read at two-thirds** and **×⅔**.
  **Back to the heard tempo** undoes it.

Either holds until the needle comes up.

## Hot cues

Eight per deck, saved with the track. The **HOTCUE** heading counts them:
**3 OF 8**. A track you haven't touched in Dub shows the hot cues it has in
Serato, rekordbox or Traktor, and the heading says whose: **FROM TRAKTOR · 3
OF 8**. Change any of them and the set becomes yours (see
[A track in more than one app](/library/import#a-track-in-more-than-one-app)).

| Do this | On |
| --- | --- |
| Click | An empty cue: sets it at the playhead. |
| Click | A set cue, deck playing: jumps there and keeps playing. |
| Hold | A set cue, deck paused: plays from it until you let go, then returns. |
| ⇧-click | A set cue: clears it. |
| Right-click | A set cue: **Rename…**, **Colour**, **Clear**. |

**Rename…** asks for a name like **INTRO**. **Colour** offers **None**,
**Red**, **Orange**, **Yellow**, **Green**, **Aqua**, **Blue**, **Purple** and
**Pink**.

New cues snap to the nearest beat. Turn that off with **Snap cue points to the
beat grid** in **Preferences ▸ Decks**.

Mapped hot-cue keys fire on the master deck. Hold **⇧** with the key to clear.

## Loops

Loops grab the beats you just heard: press **4** and the last four beats
repeat, the playhead jumping back into them. They need the track's beat grid.

- Lengths run from **1/8** to **16** beats, four on screen at a time. At rest
  you see **1 · 2 · 4 · 8**; **‹** and **›** show shorter or longer lengths.
- While a loop runs, those buttons become **÷2** and **×2**. They halve or
  double the running loop and keep its start.
- Press the lit length again to leave the loop. Playback carries on from
  there.

## Echo out

**ECHO OUT** throws the deck into its echo, 100 % wet. Tap it on the beat you
want to throw away: that beat plays, then repeats and decays while the dry
signal is cut. The deck keeps playing underneath. Tap again and the tune comes
back where it has got to.

It works on THRU too. Loading a track or switching the source drops a running
echo. Turn the button off in **Preferences ▸ FX ▸ Echo out**.

If the deck's output meter reads **ECHO OUT**, the tail has died and the deck
is silent. Tap it off.

<Note>
**Alpha.** With **Dub FX channel** on in **Preferences ▸ Alpha**, the button
becomes **OUT** beside a **HOLD** toggle. HOLD sustains the thrown beat
through a record swap; tap HOLD again to let it decay, or **OUT** to cut it.
</Note>

## Instant doubles

Drag a deck's title or artist onto the other deck. The track lands there at
the same position, in sync, replacing what was loaded. You can also give
each direction a key; see [Actions with no button](/map-mode#actions-with-no-button).

## When the needle skips

A brief dropout from dust or a skip is held: the deck carries on at the speed
it had. If the signal stays gone, the deck pauses where it was.

To keep the music going while you fix the needle, switch the deck to **INT**:
the track plays on its own clock from exactly where it was. Then recalibrate
and hand it back to **TC**, step by step in
[Saving a set on stage](/troubleshooting#saving-a-set-on-stage).
