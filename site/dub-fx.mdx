<Warning>
  Everything on this page is alpha. It works, but it isn't finished and it
  will change. It's off by default; leave it off at a gig.
</Warning>

## Turn it on

Open **Preferences ▸ Alpha**. Turn on **Alpha features**, then the feature
you want: **Dub FX channel** or **Following beat grid**. Turn **Alpha
features** off and every one of them is gone from the surface again.

## The DUB FX channel

With **Dub FX channel** on, each deck's source switch gets a fourth position:
**INT · TC · THRU · DUB FX**. Click **DUB FX** and the deck stops being a
turntable. Its track is unloaded and the record is replaced by a rack of four
vintage units, working on whatever your mixer sends it.

<img src="/images/dub-fx.png" alt="A deck on DUB FX: the Big Knob, phaser, Space Echo and spring units stacked as a rack, with the input lane beside them" />

To go back, click **INT**, **TC** or **THRU** on the same switch.

### Patch it on the mixer

Dub doesn't route anything inside itself. The mixer does:

- **In:** patch the mixer's aux send or FX loop into the input pair that
  deck's needle used.
- **Out:** the rack returns on that deck's output pair, into a mixer channel.

Anything you send from the mixer goes through the rack: the other deck, the
MC's mic, the siren. On a battle mixer with no send, plug a mic straight into
the interface instead.

### The input lane

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **SEND** / **MIC** | **SEND** for the mixer's send. **MIC** for a mic straight into the interface; it puts the mic in both channels. |
| **TRIM** | Input gain, −24 to +24 dB. |
| The VU | The input level after the trim. |
| **● HOT** | The input peaked within 1 dB of full scale. Turn the trim down. |

### The rack

The channel has one mode, **EXPERT**: every knob is the unit's own control.
The line under the title shows the signal path, each unit lit while it's in.
The units run top to bottom:

| Unit | What it is | Controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **BIG KNOB** | A high-pass filter, 12 dB/oct, 11 steps from 70 Hz to 7.5 kHz | The dial, **Q** |
| **PHASER** | A 6-stage stereo phaser | **RATE**, **DEPTH**, **FEEDBACK**, **MIX** |
| **SPACE ECHO** | A tape echo with reverb | **MODE**, **REPEAT**, **INTENSITY**, **ECHO**, **REVERB**, **W&F**, and **HOLD**, **SYNC**, **TAP** |
| **SPRING** | A spring reverb tank | **DECAY**, **TONE**, **WET**, and **KICK** to kick the tank |

Each unit has an **IN**/**OUT** toggle. The channel starts with all four out.
A unit that's out stays readable, so you can set it up first and then throw
it in.

On the **SPACE ECHO**, **INTENSITY** past 1.0 makes the tape feed itself and
the unit says **RUNAWAY**.

### SYNC and TAP

**SYNC** puts the Space Echo's repeats in time with the music. It takes the
tempo from the other deck when that deck has one; otherwise from what you
**TAP**. The unit shows where the tempo comes from, or **No tempo · tap**.

- **½**, **¾** and **1** set the longest echo to that much of a beat.
- **TAP** the beat a few times. A gap of more than two seconds starts a new
  count.
- Turning **REPEAT** by hand takes **SYNC** off.

### Space Echo HOLD

**HOLD** on the Space Echo stops anything new going onto the tape and keeps
the loop going, so the echo carries the room while you swap the record. Press
it again to let the tape fade.

### The siren and samples into the rack

When the siren or the samples are sent to the deck that's the FX channel,
their output pill reads **→ FX**: the siren plays through the rack.

## HOLD on a deck's echo

With **Dub FX channel** on, each deck's echo gets a **HOLD** button beside
it, and **ECHO OUT** reads **OUT**. **Echo out** must be on in
**Preferences ▸ FX**.

This is the one-turntable dub transition:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Throw the echo">
    Press **OUT** on the record that's playing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press HOLD">
    The last beat sustains instead of dying away. **HOLD** only works while
    the echo is on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap the record">
    Lift the needle and put the next record down while the echo holds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it go">
    Press **HOLD** again to let the echo decay, or **OUT** to cut it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Both **HOLD** buttons, the rack's **IN**/**OUT** toggles, **TAP** and
**KICK** can take keys in [map mode](/map-mode).

## Following beat grid

For records played by a band. With **Following beat grid** on, a track whose
beats wander off any even grid (live reggae, soul, rock) gets a line on each
beat where the band played it, worked out when the track loads. A steady
track keeps its even grid.

Nothing is saved. Turn it off and load the track again to get the even grid
back.
