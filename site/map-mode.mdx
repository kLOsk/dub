## Nothing you play has a key

Dub ships with two keys bound, and both are app conventions:

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Space** | Loads the library selection onto the deck that isn't the master. |
| **⌘,** | Opens Preferences. |

Hot cues, the sampler, Quick Scratch, the siren, echo out: everything you
play starts with no key. Your hands know their own keys, so you map them
yourself.

## Map a control

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn MAP on">
    Click **MAP** in the top bar. It lights while map mode is on. Every
    control you can bind shows a dashed ring and its key cap, empty if
    nothing is bound yet. The controls don't fire while MAP is on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a control">
    The ring goes solid and the control says **PRESS A KEY**. Click it
    again to let it go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press the key">
    The key is bound and its cap prints on the control. Map the next
    control, or leave map mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click MAP to leave">
    The controls play again, each with its key on it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While a control waits for a key:

- **Escape** backs out without changing anything.
- **⌫** leaves the control with no key.
- A key with **⌘** held is its own binding: **⌘J** and **J** are two
  different keys.
- A modifier on its own (⇧, ⌥, ⌃, ⌘) isn't a key; Dub waits for the real one.

**MAP** is on the **Performance** surface only. It isn't there in **Prep**,
or while you record a side.

## One key, one control

If you press a key that another control already has, the key moves: the new
control gets it and the old one is left with no key. That includes
**Space**, so you can give it to something else.

**⌘,** can't be moved or taken. It's always the way back into Preferences.

## What you can map

| Where | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| Each deck | The hot cue pads, the Quick Scratch pads, **ECHO OUT**, key **LOCK**, and the **BPM**, to tap the 1 and the tempo |
| The rack bar | The siren's five shots, the sampler pads |
| The DUB FX channel ([alpha](/dub-fx)) | Each unit's **IN**/**OUT** toggle, the Space Echo's **HOLD** and **TAP**, the spring's **KICK**, and **HOLD** on each deck's echo |

## Actions with no button

Some actions have no control on the surface to click, so you map them in
**Preferences ▸ Keys ▸ Actions with no button**. Click the action's cap; it
says **PRESS A KEY**. Press the key. Escape, ⌫ and a key that's already
taken work as they do on the surface.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Load into the left deck (A)**, **Load into the right deck (B)** | The library selection onto that deck. Refused while the deck plays, as for Space. |
| **Load into the deck not playing** | What **Space** does: onto the deck that isn't the master. |
| **Instant double A → B**, **B → A** | The other deck's track, at its playhead. Dragging a deck's title still works too. |
| **Tap the grid A**, **B** | Puts the 1 at that deck's playhead. Hold **⇧** to halve the tempo, **⌥** to double it. Does nothing on a locked grid. |
| **Loop off A**, **B** | Ends that deck's loop, without finding the ✕ on the loop row. |
| **Jump to search** | Puts the cursor in the library's search field. |
| **Next track**, **Previous track** | Moves the library selection one row. With nothing selected, it starts at the top or the bottom. |

<Tip>
  Map next track to a key that types nothing, such as **↓** or an F-key.
  It then works from inside the search field too: jump to search, type, press
  next track to land on the first result, and Space loads it.
</Tip>

## Modifiers

**⇧** and **⌥** aren't keys of their own. Held with a bound key, they change
what it does:

- **⇧** with a hot cue's key clears that cue.
- **⇧** with **Tap the grid** halves the tempo; **⌥** doubles it.

**⌘** is different: **⌘J** and **J** are two separate keys, each bound to its
own action.

## See and reset your map

**Preferences ▸ Keys** lists every bound control with its key, under
**BOUND NOW**. You map on the surface, not there.

**Reset all bindings…** at the bottom of that list puts everything back:
every control you mapped loses its key, and **Space** and **⌘,** come back.
It asks first.

Dub saves your map on this Mac as you make it and keeps it across launches.

## MIDI
