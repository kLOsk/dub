Prep is the work you do before the gig, on the couch with no rig attached.
It is one deck, playing on your Mac's own output, with the library below
it. Every control in Prep is meant to be clicked with the mouse: you
prepare a track and test it.

![The Prep surface: one deck, the hot cues and samples under the waveform, the library below](/images/prep.png)

## Getting to Prep

Dub picks the surface from your hardware:

- **No DJ interface plugged in**: Dub opens Prep.
- **A DJ interface plugged in**: Dub opens the two-deck **Performance**
  surface. Plug one in while you're in Prep and Dub moves to Performance;
  unplug it and Dub falls back to Prep.

### Switching by hand

With **Vinyl recording** on in **Preferences ▸ Vinyl**, the top bar shows a
**PREP · REC · PERF** switch. It lets you go into Prep without unplugging
your interface. **PERF** only shows while a DJ interface is connected, and
**REC** is the way into [ripping a record](/rip).

## What's on the surface

From top to bottom:

- **The deck header**: title and artist, **BPM** to two decimals, **KEY**, a
  Play/Pause button, and both the elapsed and the remaining time.
- **The overview**: the whole track. Click anywhere to jump there.
- **The playing waveform**, running left to right across the full width.
  Drag it to scratch-cue: drag left to play forward, right to play
  backwards, hold still for silence.
- **HOTCUE** and **SAMPLES**, side by side.
- **The library**, with the rest of the window.

## Loading a track

Drag a track onto the deck, or select it in the library and press
**Space**. In Prep the track starts playing as soon as it loads.

Prep always lets you load onto the deck, even while it plays. There is no
audience to protect.

## Setting cues

The **HOTCUE** bank has eight pads. An empty pad says **set at playhead**.

| Do this | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Click an empty pad | Sets a cue at the playhead. |
| Click a set pad while the deck plays | Jumps to the cue. |
| Hold a set pad while the deck is paused | Plays from the cue until you let go, then returns to it. |
| **⇧**-click a set pad | Clears it. |
| Right-click a set pad | **Rename…**, **Colour** or **Clear**. |

Cues are saved with the track and show up on the same track in
Performance. Hot cues, loops and the beat grid are covered in
[Decks](/performance/decks).

## Fixing the grid

There is no beat-grid editor. To move the grid, set the 1 from the deck
header's BPM; see [Decks](/performance/decks).

## Loading samples

**SAMPLES** is the sampler's eight slots. Drag a track from the library or
a file from Finder onto a slot to load it, then click the slot to hear it.
Right-click a quiet slot to rename it, tag it for Quick Scratch or unload
it. The same slots fire in Performance; see
[Samples and siren](/performance/samples-and-siren).

## What Prep doesn't have

- **One deck only.** Deck B is off.
- **No FX.** Echo out, the siren and Quick Scratch are things you do to a
  record in front of people, so they live in Performance.
- **No map mode.** **MAP** is a Performance control; Prep is worked with
  the mouse.
- **No rack bar** under the deck.

<Note>
  Prep plays on the Mac's own output, not on your interface. To hear a
  record through the rig, use Performance.
</Note>
