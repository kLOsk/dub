# Dub 1.0.0-alpha.3

The third alpha is about **the stage**: what to do when the needle breaks,
why the fader is up and nothing comes out, and running Dub on an interface
it has never seen. And Dub has a **[manual](https://dub-3ccc829c.mintlify.site)** now.

> **Still an alpha.** Try it at home, in the studio, at a practice session.
> Don't take it to a paying gig yet — and please tell us what breaks
> ([rig checklist](https://github.com/kLOsk/dub/blob/v1.0.0-alpha.3/docs/RIG-CHECKLIST.md)).

## Download

**`Dub-1.0.0-alpha.3-macOS-universal.zip`** below — one app for Apple Silicon
and Intel, macOS 13 or later.

1. Unzip it and drag **Dub** into **Applications**.
2. Open it. This build is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS stops it the
   first time: **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, find *"Dub" was
   blocked*, click **Open Anyway**. Once.
   (Or: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Dub.app`.)
3. Allow microphone access when asked — it is how macOS names the audio
   interface's inputs.

## What's new

**The stage rescue**
- The needle breaks mid-set: switch the deck to **INT** and the track plays on
  from exactly where it was. INT stays until you switch back.
- **↻ works on INT** — fix the needle or swap the record, and recalibrate while
  the set plays on.
- A lamp in the TC segment says when the record is ready to take the deck
  back: amber while it measures, green when it's ready. Press **TC** and the
  record picks the track up where INT has it.

**Every deck's output, metered**
- Two slim meters under the phase meter show what each deck actually sends to
  the mixer — after everything, echo tail included.
- An empty meter says why: **ECHO OUT** (echo out is still on and its tail has
  died away) or **NO OUTPUT**.

**The signal panel speaks DJ**
- One word for the timecode — **LOCKED**, **WEAK**, **NO LOCK**,
  **NO SIGNAL**, **NO INPUT** — and one line of what to check, instead of the
  decoder's numbers.
- Input levels in dB, and the carrier drawn as a circle: a weak channel
  squashes it. Timecode decks are metered now, not only THRU.

**Any interface, routed by you**
- **Preferences ▸ Audio** shows the interface and each deck's pair in and out.
  A known box (the SL 3) is read-only, marked **KNOWN · TESTED**; any other
  gets pickers — one deck or two, a meter and a word on each input
  (timecode / music / nothing), and **TEST** for a short 1 kHz tone on a
  deck's pair.
- Saved per interface. Before this, an interface Dub didn't know fell back to
  one deck on inputs 1+2.

**Sample pads**
- A sampler or Quick Scratch pad whose file was moved or deleted says
  **MISSING** (or **UNREADABLE**) instead of looking like it's still loading.
  Quick Scratch won't park your tune for silence.
- **Rename…** on a pad's right-click names the slot.

**The top bar**
- The clock and battery sit in the middle; an error stays up 10 seconds.

**The manual**
- [dub-3ccc829c.mintlify.site](https://dub-3ccc829c.mintlify.site): install,
  hardware, your first set, the deck, samples and the siren, Prep, ripping,
  map mode, preferences, the library, and troubleshooting.
- How to get a Lexicon library into Dub is documented there too.

## Fixed

- The universal Release build no longer times out type-checking the DUB dial.
- Hints that sent you to Preferences tabs that don't exist ("Recording",
  "Libraries") now name the real ones (**Vinyl**, **Library**), and the
  Real Records list says to rip with **REC** in the top bar.

## Known issues

- Not yet confirmed on the rig: the stage rescue, audio routing on an
  interface other than the SL 3, the new signal panel, and whether a spent
  echo out reads **ECHO OUT** on the meter.
- Serato 4/5's library (stored in SQLite) is not read yet. Use the rekordbox
  XML route instead (see the manual's Lexicon page).
- The 1 of a beat grid can only be set in Prep; in Performance there is no
  key or control for it yet.
- Drum'n'bass on THRU still reads at two-thirds of its tempo until you
  right-click ×1.5 or tap it.
- Not notarized (see Download). macOS only.

## Requirements

- macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
- For Performance: an audio interface with at least 4 inputs and 4 outputs,
  control vinyl, and an external mixer. Serato SL 3 is the reference rig.
