# Dub 1.0.0-alpha.1

**The first public build of Dub** — timecode vinyl for scratch DJs and sound
systems. Two turntables, your own mixer, real records through the software.

> **This is an alpha.** It plays, scratches, loops, rips and dubs on real
> hardware, and it is still being signed off on the rig one session at a time
> ([checklist](https://github.com/kLOsk/dub/blob/v1.0.0-alpha.1/docs/RIG-CHECKLIST.md)).
> Try it at home, in the studio, at a practice session. Don't take it to a
> paying gig yet — and please tell us what breaks.

## Download

**`Dub-1.0.0-alpha.1-macOS-universal.zip`** below — one app for Apple Silicon
and Intel, macOS 13 or later.

1. Unzip it and drag **Dub** into **Applications**.
2. Open it. This build is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS stops it the
   first time: **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, find *"Dub" was
   blocked*, click **Open Anyway**. Once.
   (Or: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Dub.app`.)
3. Allow microphone access when asked — it is how macOS names the audio
   interface's inputs.

Without an interface Dub opens in **Prep** (one deck, built-in output). With a
4-in / 4-out interface such as the Serato SL 3 it becomes the two-deck
**Performance** surface.

## What's in it

**Timecode that follows the hand**
- Serato CV02, Traktor MK1 and Traktor MK2 control vinyl, clean-room decoded,
  relative and absolute.
- INT · TC · THRU per deck, Panic Play, and symmetric forward / backward
  playback for scratching and rewinds.
- Calibrates itself on the needle — and names a failing cartridge channel or
  cable instead of playing badly.
- Key lock from a pure-Rust time-stretcher.

**Real records through the software**
- THRU passes a record straight through the engine with a **live waveform**
  and a **live tempo** — dim while it listens, solid once it is sure. Tap the
  BPM to set it yourself; the tap holds until the needle comes up.

**Waveforms, cues, loops**
- Metal-rendered multi-colour waveforms, beat grids with set-the-1, BPM and
  Camelot key for every track, a one-beat phase meter between the decks.
- 8 named, coloured hot cues; loops from 1/8 to 16 beats, manual in/out and
  reverse grab.
- Sampler, Quick Scratch, instant doubles, and map mode — nothing ships
  pre-bound, click a control and press a key.

**One-turntable dub**
- Echo Out with **HOLD**: throw the last beat, hold it through the record swap.
- The **DUB FX** channel on your mixer's send: Big Knob high-pass, phaser, a
  Space Echo with tape heads, wow & flutter, SYNC and TAP, and a spring tank
  you can kick.
- The **dub siren**: five shots through their own echo, one DUB knob.

**Rip your vinyl**
- Drop the needle; Dub records the side, splits the tracks, and hands you a
  tracklist. Finish to get tagged, analysed FLAC in your library.
  Recognition (AcoustID, MusicBrainz, Discogs) is optional; the rip never needs
  the network.

**Your library, not ours**
- Import Serato (crates, grids, cues), Traktor, rekordbox XML, Apple Music.
- Crates, smart crates, ratings, colours, filters, play history, missing-file
  relocation, fingerprint dedupe.
- Export rekordbox XML and M3U8.

## Fixed since the last rig session

- ECHO OUT works on a THRU record — the one-turntable throw starts there.
- Echo Out's first repeat is already quieter than the beat, not the same beat
  twice.
- Timecode calibration only measures a turning record: no more robotic
  playback after a stopped needle or a THRU record was measured by mistake.
- A missing sampler file is a quiet notice, not a red banner at every launch.
- The Space Echo's knobs no longer walk sideways with their values (and no
  longer stall the waveforms while SYNC follows the pitch).

## Known issues

- **Drum'n'bass on THRU reads at two-thirds of its tempo** (≈116 for ≈175).
  Tap the BPM to override. Library analysis shares the read.
- Not yet confirmed on the rig: Space Echo HOLD / SYNC / TAP through a mixer
  send, the THRU tempo and live waveform, a full rip inside the app, and the
  hour-long soak.
- Traktor MK2 vinyl and the Native Instruments Audio 6 are unverified on real
  hardware.
- Not notarized (see Download). macOS only.

## Requirements

- macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
- For Performance: an audio interface with at least 4 inputs and 4 outputs,
  control vinyl, and an external mixer. Serato SL 3 is the reference rig.
