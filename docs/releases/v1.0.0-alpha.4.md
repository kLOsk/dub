# Dub 1.0.0-alpha.4

The fourth alpha is about **your own library and your hands**. Dub now
shows the hot cues you set in Traktor, Serato or rekordbox. It ships
with a keyboard map that plays both decks. And the first launch walks
you through checking your rig, needle drop included.

> **Still an alpha.** Try it at home, in the studio, at a practice session.
> Don't take it to a paying gig yet, and please tell us what breaks
> ([rig checklist](https://github.com/kLOsk/dub/blob/v1.0.0-alpha.4/docs/RIG-CHECKLIST.md)).

## Download

**`Dub-1.0.0-alpha.4-macOS-universal.zip`** below: one app for Apple Silicon
and Intel, macOS 13 or later.

1. Unzip it and drag **Dub** into **Applications**.
2. Open it. This build is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS stops it the
   first time: **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, find *"Dub" was
   blocked*, click **Open Anyway**. You only do this once.
   (Or: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Dub.app`.)
3. Follow the welcome guide (below).

## What's new

**Your DJ app's library, cues included**
- A track you prepared in Traktor, Serato or rekordbox shows that app's
  hot cues on Dub's pads. The cue heading says where they came from:
  **FROM TRAKTOR · 3 OF 8**.
- When a track is in more than one app, **Preferences ▸ Library** explains
  whose beat grid and whose cues win.
- Dub reads those apps and **never writes to them**. Your first change to
  an imported cue makes the set Dub's own; Traktor's copy stays as it was.

**A keyboard map that plays**
- Dub starts with keys for both decks. Your left hand plays deck A and your
  right hand deck B:
  - hot cues on **1–5** / **6–0** (**⇧** clears one);
  - tap the BPM on **Q** / **P** (**⇧** halves, **⌥** doubles);
  - loop off on **W** / **O**, echo out on **T** / **Y**;
  - Quick Scratch on **A S D F** / **H J K L**;
  - samples 1–8 on **Z X C V B N M ,**.
- Loading and doubling:
  - **⇧←** / **⇧→** load the selection into deck A / B;
  - **⌃⇧←** / **⌃⇧→** double the other deck's track onto it;
  - **Space** loads into the empty deck.
- The library from the keyboard:
  - **↑** / **↓** move through the tracks;
  - **⌘F** jumps to search; type, then **↓** lands on the first result;
  - **⌘⇧↑** / **⌘⇧↓** move through the playlists, and **⌘⇧←** / **⌘⇧→**
    close and open folders.
- Every key moves: click **MAP**, then a control, then the key.
  **Preferences ▸ Keys** maps the actions with no button. A key you mapped
  in an earlier alpha keeps working.

**A welcome guide that checks your rig**
- It shows the interface Dub found and whether its routing is ready, or
  lets you route it right there.
- It explains the microphone permission before macOS asks for it.
- Drop the needle and each deck turns green when its timecode arrives.
- It turns on the DJ apps found on this Mac, or imports a folder.
- It shows the key map, and gives you the source switch in three lines.

**Tap the BPM on stage**
- In Performance and on THRU, tapping a deck's BPM sets the 1 as well as
  the tempo, unless its grid is locked. Right-click the BPM for 2×, ½,
  reset or the lock.

**The library**
- Every sidebar section folds, and so do your iTunes / Apple Music
  folders. A folder's number counts its playlists.
- The BPM column carries one **⚠** instead of two marks.
- *"↝ usually: …"*, the track you usually play next, now sits on the
  Performance deck. **Preferences ▸ Decks** turns it off.

**Following beat grid (alpha, off by default)**
- A followed grid is saved with the track. Its BPM reads in cyan, and once
  you lock it the padlock turns cyan too. Hover the BPM for the band's
  tempo range.

## Fixed

- Clicking a loop no longer slides that loop button sideways.
- A tap on the grid of a following deck no longer flattens it to an even
  grid for the rest of the load.
- The welcome guide no longer says audio needs no setup, and no longer
  offers only a folder import.

## Known issues

- Not yet confirmed on the rig: imported Traktor cue positions on MP3s
  (an encoder-delay offset is possible), the welcome guide's signal check,
  and the **⌃⇧** arrows. macOS uses **⌃←**/**⌃→** for Spaces; if a double
  does nothing, map it to another key.
- The library upgrades the first time alpha.4 opens it. Going back to an
  earlier alpha afterwards isn't supported.
- Serato 4/5's library (stored in SQLite) is not read yet. Use the rekordbox
  XML route instead (see the manual's Lexicon page).
- Drum'n'bass on THRU still reads at two-thirds of its tempo until you
  right-click ×1.5 or tap it.
- Not notarized (see Download). macOS only.

## Requirements

- macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
- For Performance: an audio interface with at least 4 inputs and 4 outputs,
  control vinyl, and an external mixer. Serato SL 3 is the reference rig.
